\begin{abstract}
We argue that overfitting is best understood as a failure to generalize
rather than a failure to memorize. This note motivates a simple
regularization heuristic and previews the argument that follows.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation}

Modern models are large enough to memorize their training data, yet we
still expect them to perform well on data they have never seen.
Understanding why generalization happens at all is the central puzzle
this document addresses.
