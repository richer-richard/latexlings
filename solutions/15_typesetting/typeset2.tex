For any two nonnegative real numbers $a$ and $b$, the arithmetic mean is
at least the geometric mean:
\[
\frac{a+b}{2} \geq \sqrt{ab}.
\]

\noindent\textit{Proof.} Since a square is never negative,
\begin{align*}
(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b})^2 &\geq 0 \\
a - 2\sqrt{ab} + b &\geq 0 \\
\frac{a+b}{2} &\geq \sqrt{ab}
\end{align*}
which is what we wanted to show.
